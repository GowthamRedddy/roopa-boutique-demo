About Us

A neighbourhood boutique for custom women's fashion

Roopa Boutique is a ladies' boutique in KPHB, Kukatpally, known for customized handwork blouses — maggam work, aari work, hand-painted designs and computer-embroidery blouses, made to your own design.

Bring us a reference, a sketch, or just an idea — we work with you to turn it into a finished piece. Saree blouse stitching and maggam job work are our speciality, and customers can reach us directly on WhatsApp to discuss designs.
